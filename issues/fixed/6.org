** FIXED #6 Fetch bounded recent history for the following feed

Resolution: account subscriptions request limit:50. Both transports collect a
bounded, ordered pre-EOSE batch before merging it into the verified-ID-deduplicated
timeline, then accept live delivery without applying the historical limit.
Ordering is timestamp ascending with descending ID ties in the GUI; the local
relay sends the reverse, as NIP-01 specifies. Mouse-wheel feed scrolling exposes
older notes. Queries repeat on reconnect, switches and follow edits; explicit
WebSocket replacement clears stale queue/dedup state so refollow restores notes.

The local relay retains its newest 256 verified events in memory. Filters still
require explicit limits, now nonnegative and totaling at most 50; OR filters get
independent historical limits and their result union is deduplicated. No durable
note store, pagination, or follow-list relay synchronization was added.

History tests exposed upstream NNG pair1-poly silently dropping at its two-message
per-peer queue, including EOSE despite socket-level send success. Added the pinned
nostril_pair1_try_send extension with explicit ownership and real EAGAIN; replay
and EOSE remain pending until accepted by the peer queue. The relay serializes
this control snapshot before processing further publications. The private API,
slow-peer tradeoff and upgrade requirements are documented in [[file:../../doc/transports.org][transports]].
