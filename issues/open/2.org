** OPEN #2 Encrypt persisted account keys

The initial account implementation deliberately stores secret.key as plaintext,
with an owner-only account directory and file. Permissions are not encryption.

Choose an encrypted-at-rest format and/or OS keyring integration, considering
NIP-49 interoperability. Define unlock and recovery behaviour, key derivation,
and account import/export before adding the encrypted representation.

Acceptance:
- Existing plaintext accounts can migrate without losing or changing identity.
- Wrong passwords and interrupted migration leave recoverable original data.
- Key material and passwords are never included in logs or diagnostics.
- Unlocked key lifetime and remaining Til memory-erasure limitations are explicit.
- Tests cover migration, wrong-password handling, reopen and cleanup.
